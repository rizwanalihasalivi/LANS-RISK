# Figure and table captions
- `extended_baselines/fixed_fifo.pdf`: Independent confirmation over 100 traffic cases per condition; nominal LANS, nominal acceleration-aware CEM MPC, predictor LQR, numerical CBF, finite-scenario robust MPC and LQR+ACC. Rows show collision and corridor completion; columns show the four scenarios. Lines average the three frozen actor seeds where applicable; shading is their minimum–maximum range, not a confidence interval. Actor-free baselines are run once on the same traffic cases. Physical command FIFO spans 0–1,000 ms. Per-seed Wilson intervals and paired Holm-adjusted tests are in the adjacent CSV files.
- `stress_study/weaker_actuation.pdf` and `denser_traffic.pdf`: Prespecified frozen-controller sensitivity checks,50 new traffic cases per condition. The two perturbations are evaluated separately. Same line/shading conventions as above. These are synthetic model-mismatch and traffic-density tests; no real-traffic guarantee follows.
- `observation_profiles.pdf`: Exploratory full-state stochastic sensor-delay evaluation with 20 new traffic cases per condition and five explicit synthetic distributions. Physical command delay is zero. Lines show seed means and shaded seed ranges. The initial state is known; subsequent observed state may be stale. This is separate from the fixed command-delay study and is not a DAROM reproduction. See per-seed rate intervals; small samples and zero events do not prove zero risk.
- `runtime.pdf`: Sequential warmed decision-time diagnostics on this cloud CPU, actor 42 where needed,20 warmup then 50 timed calls per condition. Bars show median and black ticks p99; the dashed 100 ms line is the simulator step duration. Timing affects neither the frozen FIFO nor plant dynamics. Samples span evolving states and resets, not repeated identical states; p99 with 50 calls is an empirical upper quantile, not a hard deadline or WCET guarantee.
- Historical/legacy `main_latency` and `ablations_latency` figures under `project/deliverables/publication_results/confirmation/` show the complete 12-controller confirmation including the prespecified ablations. Seed-range shading is descriptive. Tables retain all losses and ties. No universal controller winner is assumed.
- `descriptive_metrics.csv`: Per condition and metric, count, sample SD, observed extrema/quantiles, mean and marginal 95% interval where supported; unavailable logger diagnostics and timing intervals are flagged explicitly. Binary quantities use fractions, not percentages. No actor-seed pooling is used for inference. Additional inherited analyses under project use their own documented bootstrap seeds and contrast/multiplicity families; their Monte Carlo intervals or adjusted p values can differ. Use results/ as the authoritative new analysis and do not interchange those inference definitions. `paired_tests.csv` reports paired a-minus-b effects for the listed baseline and ablation contrasts, bootstrap intervals, raw p values and Holm p values within each study. `lans_mpc_summary.csv` averages actor-seed condition means only for display.

- `publication_tables.pdf`/`.tex` in each study: per-scenario LANS actors 42/43/44 and actor-free nominal MPC tables. Each row retains its own paired traffic sample, n=100/50/20 for main/robustness/observation studies. Binary rates use marginal Wilson 95% intervals. RMSE is mean (sample SD) when present; no RMSE is invented for observation delay. LaTeX requires booktabs/longtable. Tables preserve failures and do not pool actor repetitions.
