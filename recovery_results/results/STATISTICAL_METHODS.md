# Statistical methods and interpretation
All condition-level estimates retain scenario, delay/profile and actor seed. Traffic seeds are paired across methods. Actor repetitions are not additional independent traffic cases; actor-free baselines are run once. Binary means/SD/CIs are fractions in descriptive_metrics.csv. Missing dense-Merge robustness conditions have no performance estimates or tests. Any partial-stage analysis uses only complete 50-case conditions and does not claim the original 264-condition plan completed. The sample SD describes episode outcomes, not the standard error. Wilson 95% intervals describe each event rate; no observed collisions does not imply zero true risk. Continuous non-timing outcome means and paired-difference intervals use 5000 percentile bootstrap draws of traffic cases with deterministic analysis seed 20261022. These intervals are marginal and not simultaneous; degenerate intervals at zero discordance do not prove equivalence. Exact paired-discordance tests apply to binary outcomes. RMSE uses two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, which require a symmetric distribution of paired differences for a location interpretation. Ties/zeros are handled by SciPy auto/wilcox; all-zero differences receive p=1. Holm correction covers all listed contrasts and outcomes within each study (LANS versus each comparator plus predictor-LQR/LQR+ACC, CBF/PPO-only and robust-LANS/actor-free or robust-MPC where available); it does not combine the historical studies or bootstrap interval coverage. Positive a-minus-b differences favor method a for completion, and favor method b for collisions/RMSE. Descriptive seed means are not extra statistical samples. Worst values are observed episode extremes, not safety bounds or WCET. The raw no_feasible_steps field is meaningful only for CBF and the nominal/robust sampled-plan LANS variants (including robust_mpc). MPC nominal, PPO-only and plain LQR methods have no such recorded flag; predictor LQR hardcodes it false. Their runner-default zeros are marked unavailable in processed descriptive tables and with a coverage flag in enriched episode files. Feasibility-flag semantics also differ between CBF and finite candidate planners, so these counts do not establish solver feasibility or support a cross-method infeasibility ranking. Metrics absent from saved raw data (including RMSE in the observation-delay study and unrecorded near-miss/jerk metrics) are unavailable and are never fabricated. Main-study per-episode timing means/SD are descriptive and their confidence intervals are not inferred under shared execution sessions. Runtime mean/SD are descriptive over 50 consecutive evolving-state calls; no independent-replicate confidence interval is inferred for that timing sample. Runtime values are host-dependent diagnostics; old and new confirmation timings are from different execution sessions and must not be treated as a controlled speed comparison. Sequential runtime_profile.json is the current-machine timing evidence.
